# Questie's code, for the simulated party member (questie-party.lua)

Copied unchanged from the Questie addon's source (https://github.com/Questie/Questie), so a simulated party
member's messages are made by Questie's own code. Used only by the scenarios here; never part of OneMoreQuest's
release (tools/release-files.txt lists the addon's own files).

- `QuestieSerializer.lua`, `QuestieStream.lua`: Questie, by the Questie team, GPL-3.0.
- `AceComm-3.0.lua`, `CallbackHandler-1.0.lua`: Ace3, by the Ace3 team, under the Ace3 licence (BSD-style), as
  bundled with Questie.
- `LibStub.lua`: public domain, as bundled with Questie.
